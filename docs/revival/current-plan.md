## Sequencing rule

macOS is the leading implementation lane. D3Import, the shared canonical/Core and Metal paths, RevivalMac, and RevivalEditor establish each Phase 1–7 source-led product slice. RevivalMobile may compile and compose proven work early through its concrete UIKit target, but required physical integration of storage, input, audio-session, lifecycle, presentation, and accumulated player behavior begins in Phase 8. This is implementation order, not reduced scope: RevivalMobile and touch remain required for version 1.0 and use the same canonical world, renderer, scheduler, package, save, replay, and applicable multiplayer paths.

Roadmap phases are dependency groupings, not global barriers for every developer. Use these evidence boundaries:

1. **Mac/shared checkpoint:** the current source contract works through D3Import or the canonical boundary, shared Core/Metal where applicable, RevivalMac, and RevivalEditor where the slice has a creator path.
2. **Early mobile compatibility evidence:** target builds, unsigned generic-device builds, focused automation, and simulator checks may prove only the mobile wiring they execute. They do not create a separate Phase 1–7 closure checkpoint.
